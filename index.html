Senior Mobile Engineer/Doha, Qatar/Practising since 2018

I build banking software that people trust with their money.

Eight years of it. Currently leading the mobile architecture of the Corporate Trade Portal at Commercial Bank of Qatar — the kind of app where a dropped frame is a bug report and a wrong decimal is an incident. I like unglamorous things — clean architecture, boring release trains, tests that fail for the right reason. Good software is invisible. You only notice mine when it isn't there.
